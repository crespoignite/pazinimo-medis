The Pažinimo medis philosophy rests on the belief that educating the whole child leads to high academic achievement. The 2025–2026 results of the national student achievement tests (NMPP) confirm it. Forty-two fourth graders from the Pažinimo medis schools in Vilnius and Kaunas showed solid academic knowledge, with results above the national average for several years in a row.

## High achievement in reading, comprehension and writing

In the 2025–2026 national Lithuanian language and literature (reading) test for fourth graders, Pažinimo medis pupils averaged 81.9%, against 67.2% for Lithuanian fourth graders overall. For comparison, the school's fourth-grade average was 86.6% in 2024–2025 and 77.3% in 2023–2024. The share of pupils with the highest results stands out: 38.1% of Pažinimo medis fourth graders reached the higher level, against 16.6% of pupils in Lithuania.

By content area, pupils did best on tasks in reading, text comprehension, and knowledge of literature and culture, with an average of 81.6%. Results in writing and text creation were very similar, at an average of 81.4%. By cognitive skill, pupils scored highest, an average of 85.1%, on tasks that demand higher-order thinking.

These results show strong achievement in Lithuanian language and literature. They also show that pupils can handle more complex tasks that call for higher-order thinking, and that their text comprehension and interpretation skills are well developed. Strong results in writing and text creation show an ability to express thoughts coherently and to write different types of text.

## Fourth graders show strong mathematical thinking

Pažinimo medis fourth graders also did very well in the national mathematics test. This year the average was 81.89%, against 68.6% for Lithuanian fourth graders overall. The high level has held steady for several years: the school's fourth-grade average was 82.5% in 2024–2025 and 87.1% in 2023–2024.

The higher mathematics level was reached by 30.2% of Pažinimo medis fourth graders and 15.8% of pupils in Lithuania. It is also good to see that 67.2% of Pažinimo medis pupils reached the basic level. In Lithuania 60.1% of fourth graders reached the basic level, 17.8% reached the satisfactory level and 2.9% did not reach the threshold. No pupil at Pažinimo medis scored below the satisfactory level in mathematics.

By content area in mathematics, Pažinimo medis fourth graders did best in data and probability, with an average of 93.3%. By cognitive skill, they did best on showing knowledge and understanding, with an average of 90.9%.

The mathematics results of our fourth graders show that pupils master the basics of mathematics, can apply what they know in different situations, solve problems and justify their solutions. Strong mathematical thinking is an important base for further learning and academic success.

The Pažinimo medis community is delighted with the fourth graders' achievements and thanks pupils, teachers and parents for their steady work and cooperation in pursuit of the best educational results.

At Pažinimo medis we aim to strengthen pupils' skills in reading, writing, mathematics and other academic areas, and also to develop emotional intelligence, social skills, critical thinking, cooperation and independence. A strong academic foundation, individual attention for every child and an environment that strengthens the motivation to learn help pupils achieve high results and grow into confident, responsible and curious people.
