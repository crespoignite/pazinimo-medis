title: The challenge of a future fifth grader: advice for parents from the head of school

A child's move from primary to basic school is one of the most significant and sensitive steps in a pupil's life. For parents it is a time to rethink their role: how much to help, and when to let the child take responsibility for their own decisions.

> Grade 5 is one of the most important stages of a child's development. It brings an academic change and also a deeply significant emotional and social transformation. Parents can really help a child prepare, so that this stage does not become a stressful test for the whole family, says education expert Raimunda Česonienė, head of the Pažinimo medis school in Kaunas.

## More teachers, more responsibility

One of the most striking changes when a child enters grade 5 is the structure of learning. A child who had one teacher's care moves into a system with many subject teachers. Each has their own rules, working style and ways of assessing. Children meet different teacher personalities and points of view, which builds flexibility, critical thinking and creativity. At this stage pupils often discover subjects that truly inspire them. Some fall in love with history, others find the secrets of biology, and others are unexpectedly fired up by mathematics or technology. Such discoveries create a positive learning experience and let children feel success, says R. Česonienė.

Still, learning content grows harder, with more theory, project work and homework, and this puts parents in a dilemma: how much responsibility to take on for the child and how much to let go. Too much control can suppress a child's independence, but stepping back completely can leave the child confused. Children mature quickly at this age, and the new learning environment pushes them to be more independent and take on more responsibility. Fifth graders learn to plan their day, look after their learning materials and remember tasks, and all of this naturally builds their independence, says R. Česonienė.

With enough support and room for the child, this stage can become the first step toward more mature, more conscious learning, which over time leads to greater self-confidence and motivation to learn.

## Changes in the child's social circle

Changes in the child's social circle are just as important. A change of class often brings a change of friends. Some friendships stay, others end and new ones form.

The head of the Pažinimo medis school in Kaunas points out that at this time it matters to children to feel accepted. In grade 5, she says, wider opportunities for self-expression open up. The community circle grows, and clubs, projects and shared activities help pupils make new friends, find like-minded people and build their identity. Parents need to understand that at this stage a child is learning to resolve conflicts, express feelings and find their place among peers. This is a natural part of development.

## How can parents help a future fifth grader?

> The greatest help for a child is their parents' calm and trust. If parents frighten them or keep stressing the difficulties, the child picks it up. If parents believe in the child, the child feels safer.
>
> Raimunda Česonienė, head of the Pažinimo medis school in Kaunas

According to the education expert, a parent's role as a child enters grade 5 is not to control but to support.

The expert advises:

- **Encourage independence every day.** Let your child pack their own bag, follow the timetable and plan homework. Help with questions, not with solutions.
- **Create a safe space for conversation.** Ask about more than grades. Ask about feelings: what brings joy and what causes worry.
- **Show trust.** Children feel their parents' emotions. Calm, trusting parents give a child a sense of safety.
- **Take an interest in how the school works.** A clear understanding of the learning process reduces anxiety and helps the child prepare for change.
- **Establish a routine.** A steady daily schedule makes it easier to adjust to a new environment.
- **Accept difficulties as part of growing.** Not every problem has to be solved for the child. What matters most is to be nearby when they need you.

A child does not need parents to do everything for them. It matters to them to know that parents are close when needed. When a child learns to overcome challenges with supportive adults nearby, they grow stronger, as a pupil and as a person, stresses R. Česonienė.

## More trust in teachers

The first months of grade 5 are a time when parents' trust in teachers and in the learning process matters especially. In Pažinimo medis schools the move from primary to progymnasium education is a period of getting to know and observing the child. Teachers first aim to see each child, their strengths and learning style, to sense the child's pace and to let them adjust to the new environment and expectations.

It is no accident that fifth graders receive no grades in the first month. Instead, a safe relationship with learning is built and self-confidence is strengthened. In October teachers help children live through their first learning successes and setbacks, teaching them to accept these as a natural part of growth. At Pažinimo medis each child's progress is compared only with their own. The final academic goal is the same for everyone, but with each child we walk a different path to reach it. That is why parents' calm and trust become a key factor that helps a child grow and learn with courage, says the head of the Pažinimo medis school in Kaunas.

A fifth grade is already forming at the school next to the VDU Botanical Garden, which opened in 2022. It is a community where every pupil gets attention, children's social and emotional competencies are strengthened and a motivating learning environment is built. The Pažinimo medis school has 19 years of experience in education and pays special attention to well-rounded education, which creates the foundation for academic achievement and pupils' later success.
