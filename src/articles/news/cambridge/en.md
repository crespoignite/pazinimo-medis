We are proud to announce that Pažinimo medis has received international Cambridge accreditation. We now belong to one of the most widely recognised international education models in the world, used by schools in more than 160 countries.

Becoming a Cambridge school means joining a global education community built on high academic standards, constant improvement and a culture of innovation. It is also a firm commitment to bring the academic challenge of Cambridge together with social and emotional competencies and the values of Pažinimo medis.

Rita Navikienė, head of the Pažinimo medis schools in Vilnius

## An academic commitment to quality education

What sets the Cambridge programme apart is its clear learning goals, set out in stages. They help build knowledge and skills step by step, and a school can adapt them to its own context. Cambridge Primary is not simply an English-language programme. It is a structured education model used around the world that builds a strong academic foundation and steadily develops thinking, enquiry and communication skills.

Cambridge fits well with Lithuania's national curriculum. It lets us combine the strengths of national education with international academic experience and the best practice in education worldwide.

First graders in the Pažinimo medis Cambridge class will study the general curriculum and also three Cambridge Primary subjects, which extend and deepen it:

- Primary English
- Primary Global Perspectives
- Primary Science

## Primary English: a strong foundation in English from grade 1

Under the general curriculum, grade 1 usually has 2 English lessons a week. In the Cambridge class there are 4. The programme gives children more consistency, more language practice and a strong base in reading and writing. A specialist English teacher will lead these lessons. She will build practical speaking skills and also deliberately develop reading, writing and first science vocabulary in English.

Cambridge Primary English is organised into core areas, called strands:

- **Reading:** builds comprehension and analysis, widens vocabulary and teaches children to recognise how texts are structured.
- **Writing:** develops clear expression of ideas, the writing of different kinds of text and accuracy of language.
- **Speaking and listening:** strengthens dialogue, argument and active listening, and builds confidence in speaking.

What does this mean for a first grader? A more natural language environment, more reading and speaking situations, a wider vocabulary and clearer, steadier progress in English.

## Primary Global Perspectives: first graders look wider

The Global Perspectives module is one lesson a week. It was chosen to grow curious, responsible, civic-minded and critical thinkers. It fits naturally with the challenge-based learning used in the Pažinimo medis primary classes, where children apply what they learn in practice. Global Perspectives is skills-based learning that deepens pupils' ability to think critically, research, collaborate and reflect. Its core is project work, which encourages active learning and meaningful use of knowledge.

Primary Global Perspectives lessons deliberately build these key skills:

- **Critical thinking:** pupils learn to ask questions, check information and compare different points of view.
- **Research skills:** children gather information, analyse it and draw reasoned conclusions.
- **Communication and collaboration:** working in groups, pupils learn to share their ideas, listen to others and present joint solutions.
- **Reflection:** conscious learning is encouraged as pupils assess what they learned and how.

The content rests on topics close to children and easy to grasp: school, family, community and environment. Pupils look at them from different perspectives and search for solutions. They come to see that even everyday questions can have a wider, global context.

## Primary Science: learning through enquiry

This module is built on the Cambridge Primary Science system, which helps pupils understand the world through observation, enquiry and hands-on experiments. Primary Science lessons run twice a week. They replace the current natural science and human studies lessons and follow the structure and method of the Cambridge programme.

The programme covers 4 main areas:

- **Scientific enquiry:** pupils learn to plan experiments, observe, collect data, and analyse and explain the results.
- **Biology:** living organisms, plants, animals and the human body.
- **Chemistry:** the properties of materials, how they change and everyday chemical processes.
- **Physics:** movement, energy, light, sound and other basic physical phenomena.

The main aim of the module is to grow curiosity, the habit of looking for scientific explanations and an understanding of the world through experience. Pupils learn more than facts. They learn a scientist's way of thinking. In Primary Science lessons children enquire, experiment and discover, and so build a firm base for scientific thinking.

## An inspiring teacher will show what the programme can do

Using the Cambridge Primary English standard shows children's abilities from the first year of the programme. Children communicate in English with more confidence because it becomes a natural tool for activity, not only a subject to study. Lessons hold more discussion, enquiry and practical tasks. Pupils are encouraged to give an answer and also to explain their thinking: why they believe something and what they base it on. They learn to reflect on their own learning.
