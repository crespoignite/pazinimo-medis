The 2025–2026 results of the basic education achievement tests (PUPP) for tenth graders show how well Pažinimo medis gymnasium students are prepared for the next stage of learning. The averages in Lithuanian language and literature and in mathematics were above the national averages, and mathematics results rose compared with last year.

This year 26 students in the second gymnasium year (grade 10) at the Pažinimo medis gymnasium took the PUPP. According to the National Education Agency, more than 29,000 tenth graders sat the test in Lithuania.

In Lithuanian language and literature our students averaged 35.0 points out of 60, above the national average of 32.7. All 26 students reached the threshold level or higher. As many as 88.5% reached the basic level or higher, and one student reached the higher level (9 points).

In mathematics our students averaged 29.9 points out of 50, while the national average was 26.7. Of the 26 students, 25 (96.2%) reached the threshold level or higher, and 84.6% reached the basic level or higher. One student earned the top, higher level (9 points).

## Compared with 2025

In 2025 the Lithuanian language and literature average at the Pažinimo medis gymnasium was 34.8 points. In 2026 it rose to 35.0. The mathematics average rose from 25.2 to 29.9 points.

These results show steady growth in student achievement and successful preparation for the basic education achievement tests. We are especially pleased that the averages in both Lithuanian language and literature and mathematics were above the national averages.
