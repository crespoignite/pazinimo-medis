## Two directions, one vision

### The Knowledge Lab

This after-school activity runs once a week in Vilnius and Kaunas. Pupils in grades 2 to 6 go deeper into different science and arts disciplines through hands-on work. They explore their strengths and ways of learning, meet professionals from many fields, and create and present the results of their work at science fairs.

### Empowering teachers

Pažinimo medis teachers learn to recognise gifted children in mixed classes. They learn methods that include everyone and leave nobody on the sidelines, including pupils who show higher potential. Thanks to the Erasmus+ project LUMOS, teachers deliberately apply the principles of Universal Design for Learning (UDL), artificial intelligence (AI) tools and STEAM methods.

So that children's potential does not go unnoticed and their motivation does not fade, the programme for gifted learners works in two directions: with children and with teachers. This creates a lasting environment where it is natural for gifted children to grow.
