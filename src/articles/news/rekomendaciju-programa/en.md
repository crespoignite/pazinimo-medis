We invite all parents in our community to help build a harmonious, values-based Pažinimo medis environment. Relationships and trust matter greatly to us. We believe that parents' own recommendations to attend Pažinimo medis are a natural and authentic way to grow Pažinimo medis together with wonderful people.

Community is one of the most important values we nurture. A recommendation is a sign of trust, and also a way to make sure our community is joined by families who share similar values.

## Terms of the referral programme

To help Pažinimo medis grow, and with great attention to community, we invite you to recommend our kindergarten or school to a family not connected with Pažinimo medis. Both families then receive a tuition discount.

- When a family attending a Pažinimo medis kindergarten or school recommends it to another family, and that family joins the community, both families receive a one-month tuition discount, at the rates of the education stage each attends.
- The discount applies to the last month attended in the school year, once the new family has attended for at least 3 months from the start date in their agreement.
- If the new family brings more than one child, or a Pažinimo medis family recommends several new families, the discounts add up.
- To make a recommendation, write to priemimas@pazinimo-medis.lt with the contact details of the family you recommend.
- Once all details are agreed and the education agreement is signed, the admissions team sends a certificate confirming the discount.

We invite you to grow Pažinimo medis together and to walk together a meaningful, values-based path of education and discovery.
