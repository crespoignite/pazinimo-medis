A new programme, Reading Journey, is starting in Kaunas for children aged 5 to 7, in pre-primary and grade 1. Its aim is to help children strengthen their reading and text comprehension and build a positive relationship with reading.

We do not want reading to become one more academic task for a child. In Reading Journey every child learns at their own pace, notices their own progress, experiences success and gradually discovers the joy of reading.

## From the first letters to understanding a text

Children arrive with very different reading skills. Some are just starting to know letters and sounds and learning to join syllables. Others already read short texts and are learning to understand them better.

So activities will be differentiated and individualised to each child's abilities and needs.

In the programme we will build:

- reading skills, from letter to word and from word to sentence and text;
- sound analysis: hearing the sounds in a word, recognising which sound or letter a word begins and ends with, and gradually learning to write down a word they hear;
- text comprehension, so a child can say not only "I have read it" but also "I understand what I read";
- spoken expression and the basics of public speaking: we will learn to tell others about a book we read, its characters and its most interesting moments, and to give our opinion;
- creativity: we will draw, invent characters, do creative tasks based on the texts we read, and explore emotions, colours and symbols;
- fine motor skills, through a variety of creative activities;
- self-confidence and motivation to learn: children will not be compared with each other. They will learn to notice their own progress.

The programme carries a simple but important message:

> I can do it. I succeed. I read and I enjoy it.

## Not only reading, but small rituals of achievement

Once a month there will be a Book Celebration, a cosy group meeting where children can be proud of what they have done, present their work or the stories they have read, and notice their progress with the teacher and set new personal goals.

Connection with the family is an important part of the programme. Once a month, parents receive individual feedback from the teacher on their child's progress: what is going well, which skills we are strengthening now and what the next personal goal could be.

## How will the sessions run?

One Reading Journey session lasts 2 hours. It includes:

- reading, sound analysis and language development activities;
- creative tasks;
- an afternoon snack;
- a review of the day and reflection.

A small group lets the teacher give attention to every child and adapt tasks to their stage.
