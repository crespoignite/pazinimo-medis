The graduates of the fourth class of the Pažinimo medis gymnasium have shown once again that steady work brings excellent results. All 12 students earned their secondary education and came through the state matura exam marathon.

The results of the 2025–2026 state matura exams (part 2) again confirm the strong academic competence of our gymnasium students. Every graduate passed the state exams they chose, and the averages in most subjects were above 70 points. Results were especially high in the exact sciences and foreign languages. Compared with 2024–2025, students made the greatest progress in informatics, history and mathematics (level A). English, geography, Lithuanian (level A) and mathematics (level B) keep similarly high averages of 70 points or more. Once the National Education Agency publishes the official 2026 national averages, we expect most of our gymnasium's results to exceed the Lithuanian average again, as in previous years.

This year we are proud of four perfect scores of 100, in mathematics, informatics and geography. Three students who sat the physics, history and French exams missed 100 by a single point and scored 99.

Subject averages are high too: chemistry 81.0, history 85.0, geography 75.6, English 73.2, Lithuanian language and literature (level A) 71.7, mathematics (level B) 70.6 and French 95.5 points.

We are proud of our graduates' persistence, knowledge and achievement, and we sincerely thank their teachers for the professionalism, dedication and daily work that helped students reach such results.

A strong academic foundation, individual attention for every child and an environment that strengthens the motivation to learn help students achieve high results and grow into confident, responsible and curious people.
